# M0-D01: Program targets and engagement facts

Status: partial product decision, 2026-09-19. Decision owner: product owner and
tech lead under the delegated product-default rule. M0-D01 remains open for
the organization's engagement facts and professional scope judgment.

| Question | Product decision and rationale |
| --- | --- |
| Program path | A program retains one identity through readiness, Type I, and Type II. These are planned stages, not claims that an examination or operating period happened. An engagement and its outcome are separate records. |
| Dates | Program dates are optional targets. A target is never promoted to an auditor-confirmed date by passage of time, stage display, or an imported note. Confirmed dates require an attributable engagement-owned decision or auditor communication. Plan revision preserves earlier targets and author attribution. |
| Category selection | A boundary records intended Trust Services categories explicitly. No optional category is inferred from a program name or target path. Selecting a category does not claim that the product supports every category-specific requirement. |
| First service scope | A boundary may carry a typed, owned unresolved service reference before the ClientService register exists. It cannot claim a governed service identity from an arbitrary UUID. The governed service and its exact boundary relationship must later be validated. |
| Milestone dates | No R1, R2, or T1 due date is invented from a program target. Milestone due dates require an agreed plan. |

These defaults preserve a usable program draft while blocking unsupported audit
claims. They do not choose the first audit firm, actual target or confirmed
dates, services, or categories beyond those explicitly selected by management.

## Open organizational decisions

- Compliance lead and readiness consultant: confirm the intended first path,
  Type I target as-of date, Type II target observation period, and R1/R2/T1
  milestone dates from an agreed plan.
- Management and service owner: list the client services for the first boundary
  and select the Trust Services categories with rationale. Privacy selection
  requires a separate lifecycle capability and support decision before a
  product claim.
- Audit firm or management's engagement owner: identify the firm and provide
  objective evidence for any confirmed engagement dates. An unknown firm or
  date remains unknown in the product.

M0-D01 closes only after those facts, rationale, owner, and date are recorded
and the dependent R1-01, R1-02, and R1-03 issue bodies are aligned.
