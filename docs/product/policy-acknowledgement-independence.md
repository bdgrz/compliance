# Policy acknowledgement recorder independence

A workforce person correlated to the acting canonical user acknowledges their
own policy personally. Historical actual Attest assignment does not prevent this
personal act. A current program manager recording for an uncorrelated workforce
person authors a management record and consumes the retained-history management
wall. Actual same-client Attest history denies that proxy recording, including
after closure or client/guest relinking. Advisory and another client's Attest
history preserve ordinary recording authority.

`PolicyAcknowledgementRecorderGuard` is a required, explicitly registered scoped
dependency. It captures the authoritative Person once into an immutable snapshot
of tenant/person identity, creation state, revision, committed stream position
and membership correlation. Its captured-snapshot evaluation never rehydrates
that correlation. The handler retains ordinary read authorization and proxy
management permission, then current audience visibility and another member's
personal-only restriction before consulting proxy history eligibility. No whole
acknowledgement contract receives the management mutation marker.

The guard grants no membership, permission or audience participation. Consumers
must supply a trusted authoritative Person capture and retain their own ordinary
authorization. Captured-snapshot evaluation supports the separately scoped
source-aware queue consumer without duplicating correlation reads. No projection
reinterpretation or queue consumer is delivered by this source slice.

History and correlation are evaluated at dispatch; no transaction spanning a
future professional acceptance and an unrelated campaign/person stream is
claimed. Public professional authority and real ratification remain gated.
Production-composed tests retain reviewed/approved policies, frozen audiences,
launched campaigns and explicitly synthetic accepted/closed history. They prove
the personal/proxy split, error order, ordinary grants/membership, retained source
effects and durable relinking snapshot semantics. Other independence and
compartment criteria under #277 remain open.
