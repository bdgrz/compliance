# Scope and responsibility management independence

Assignment/revocation of a client record's operational responsibilities and
freezing/amendment of a program scope snapshot author client management records.
These four concrete requests consume the supplemental canonical-person Attest
history wall. Same-client actual history survives assignment closure and later
client/guest relinking; Advisory and other-client history preserve ordinary
management authority.

The shared RBAC and program request interfaces remain unmarked. Existing tenant
membership, activity, RBAC/program grants, exact source version, active assignee,
separation-of-duties and snapshot integrity/lineage checks remain required.
Snapshot reads, manifest regeneration and verification preserve their ordinary
authority. Native bootstrap and source reaction requests retain their existing
classification and authority.

Production-composed tests seed real draft and independently reviewed/approved
boundaries, registered assignee source records and a program, project retained
events through their canonical Fitz checkpoints, then dispatch actual initial
assignment and scope freezing before history. Denied writes retain those source
effects; allowed writes produce attributable events. Snapshot verification and
manifest reads remain usable after historical Attest assignment. The accepted
assignment/rule proof in these tests is explicitly synthetic.

The guard evaluates retained history at authorization; it grants no management
or professional authority and makes no cross-stream atomic assignment/write
claim. Other management consumers, professional authority, real ratification and
compartment criteria under #277 remain open.
