# Risk management mutation consumers

Fifteen concrete public risk commands consume the existing canonical retained
actual-Attest history wall: draft creation/revision, method publication,
assessment, owner designation, treatment choice, control-treatment proposal,
review and retirement, action creation/revision/cancellation, completion
submission/review, and risk acceptance. Current owning-client membership,
program grants and existing operating duties remain independently mandatory.

`AcceptRisk` remains an attributable personal HTTP-only sign-off. Its decision
accepts a client's residual risk as management; this personal transport rule
does not exempt it from the management authorship wall. Existing owner/reviewer
separation of duties and explicit waiver requirements still apply.

Risk reads and histories remain unmarked. The internal
`RaiseRiskReassessmentTriggers` reaction remains unmarked and retains its
system-only authorizer. No mixed management/read interface is broadly marked.

Production-composed tests share actual retained risk, method, control and
assessment events from the existing governance fixture. Valid treatment and
personal acceptance requests reproduce the historical-Attest bypass before the
markers, then deny it afterward; shared governance reads, Advisory/other-client
history and independent review remain allowed. Assignment history fixtures use
explicitly synthetic verified professional proof and rules.

This subset adds no public professional acceptance operation, professional
access grant, duty designation or real rule ratification. The compound criteria
and cross-stream acceptance/write concurrency remain open.
