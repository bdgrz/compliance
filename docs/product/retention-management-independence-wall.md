# Client retention management mutation consumers

The three current personal Org Admin decisions for retained evidence/import
sources consume the canonical actual-Attest write wall: recording retention
basis, placing a legal hold and releasing that hold. These are client-authored
management decisions, not a new professional engagement hold or disposition
approval. Current client membership, Org Admin permission, source ownership,
exact digest/revision and personal HTTP invocation remain mandatory.

The broader `IArtifactRetentionAdminRequest` remains unmarked because it also
carries reads. Historical Attest users retain ordinarily authorized retention
reads and MCP queries. Advisory history and Attest history for another client
do not deny the client's retention management decisions.

Production-composed tests use real evidence registration and retention events.
All three actual source decisions succeeded despite retained closed Attest
history before the markers; afterward they deny without changing retention
revision. Read assessment still reports no disposition authority. Tests also
preserve native basis dates, Advisory/other-client positives, ordinary grant
denial and direct/MCP mutation refusal.

Professional independence history/evaluation and engagement lifecycle operations
remain separately authorized governance work. They stay unmarked so an
explicitly authorized client administrator can log impairment or close an
engagement. Management responsibility acknowledgement already denies actual
Attest history through its own policy boundary.

No byte-store hold enforcement, delete/disposition authority, public professional
acceptance or real rule ratification is delivered. Compound acceptance remains
open, including cross-stream acceptance/write concurrency.
