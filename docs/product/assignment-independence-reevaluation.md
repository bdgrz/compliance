# Retained assignment revocation and closure receipts

This next bounded #277 subset attaches a causal denial-only receipt to the existing same-ledger actual-assignment removal and accepted-engagement closure paths. Actual `CloseServiceEngagement` dispatch uses the production request bus and existing current client administration authority. `RemoveActualStaff` remains an internal aggregate operation; its tests use the production aggregate executor with explicitly synthetic accepted authority. No public partner acceptance or removal adapter is introduced.

The cause and receipt are validated before either event is raised, then persisted as one optimistic-concurrency transaction. The receipt identifies its exact causal request/source sequence, selected assignment or whole-engagement closure, reason, logical intent and actor/time. It freezes the exact previous and resulting accepted snapshots with lowercase SHA-256 hashes under the typed core JSON contract, plus the full permanent canonical-person assignment history for the client. Receipt identity is UUIDv5 under the causal request, engagement, previous revision and both snapshot hashes. Replay recomputes every field from the exact retained lifecycle cause and refuses altered snapshots, history, attribution, source sequence or authority flags.

A receipt always states `review_required` and `production_acceptance_blocked = true`. This is retained evidence of a changed lifecycle source, never a fresh professional policy evaluation, accepted proof, partner approval or grant. Original frozen rules and partner evidence remain attributed to their original accepted snapshot. Existing granular lifecycle semantics remain authoritative: removing a nonlead blocks that assignee while leaving unaffected staff eligible; removing the lead or closing an engagement blocks its team. An overlapping unaffected engagement keeps its own accepted revision and eligibility. Receipts add no blanket eligibility rule and cannot restore removed/closed access. Permanent canonical history is never truncated or erased.

The additive immutable `assignment_reevaluations` collection appears on the existing authorized client independence history; historical JSON defaults it to empty. Nested previous/resulting proof and canonical assignment collections are immutable. Original source responses and attributed idempotent retry semantics remain unchanged. History sequence includes both cause and receipt physical events; callers reload the authoritative sequence before another mutation. A changed actor or logical intent cannot reuse the earlier decision.

The complete cause-plus-receipt payload is bounded to 48 KiB and cumulative assignment receipts to 1,000. Cause chronology cannot predate any canonical accepted lifecycle source or retained service facts captured by the receipt. Oversized or invalid transactions append neither lifecycle change nor receipt. Production writer failure and source OCC races cannot expose a partial closure or receipt.

Service-trigger receipts are delivered separately in #859. Acceptance-trigger adapters, cross-stream rule/directory change sources, wider missing professional/compartment consumers, real partner designation and real ratification remain separate prerequisites. No fresh professional acceptance, E2E, deployment or forecast reduction is inferred; the compound parent criteria remain open.
